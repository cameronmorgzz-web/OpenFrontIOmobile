# Wiring guide

There are two diagrams:

- [`wiring_launcher.svg`](wiring_launcher.svg) for the launcher.
- [`wiring_button.svg`](wiring_button.svg) for the button.

Both are generated by `python electronics/make_wiring.py`. The pin details are in [`PINMAP.md`](PINMAP.md).

**Safety (Italy/EU):** everything in this project is extra-low voltage (12 V DC and below). Mains is only ever inside the
CE-marked Mean Well GST60A12 desktop brick, a sealed and certified unit. **Never open the PSU, never cut its cables, and
never wire anything to mains.** If the brick or its cable is damaged, replace it. Unplug the brick before any rework.

## 1. Power tree (launcher)

```
Mains ─┤ Mean Well GST60A12 (12 V 5 A, CE, sealed) ├─ 5.5x2.1 plug
                                                   │
                                     J1 panel jack (rated ≥ 5 A)
                                                   │ 1.0 mm²
                                     F1 5 A ATO blade fuse (inline holder)
                                                   │
                                     Q1 IRF4905 P-MOSFET, reverse-polarity protection
                                       (R1 10k G→GND, ZD1 15 V zener G–S)
                                                   │
                                     TB1 star point (+12V_PROT / GND)
          ┌────────────────────────────┬───────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────────┐
     1.0 mm²                       0.5 mm²                      0.25 mm²               (GND star)
  C1 1000 µF 25 V + D1 SMBJ15A  U1 MP1584 → 5.0 V            Q2 AO3400 low side
  IBT-2 B+/B- → M1 37GB555       │ +5V_SERVO (C2 1000 µF)      12 V buzzer + beacon
  (C4 100 nF on motor tabs)      ├─ SERVO_ESC (MG996R)          (D2 1N5819 across)
                                 ├─ SW_SAFETY_PIN pole B (NC) ─ SERVO_LATCH (MG996R)
                                 └─ D3 1N5819 → +5V_LOGIC (C3 470 µF)
                                        ├─ ESP32-C3 SuperMini 5V pin → on-board LDO → 3V3
                                        └─ 2 x TCRT5000 modules
                 3V3 (SuperMini) → IBT-2 VCC/R_EN/L_EN, motor encoder, pull-ups
```

**Reverse-polarity choice: IRF4905, not SS54.** At 5 A a Schottky drops about 0.5 V and dissipates about 2.5 W, which
needs a heatsink and loses voltage at the motor. The IRF4905 (about 20 mΩ) dissipates about 0.5 W in TO-220 with no
heatsink. ZD1 is needed because a transient clamped by D1 at about 24 V would otherwise exceed Q1's ±20 V gate rating.

**Why D3:** the ESP32 is fed from the servo buck through a Schottky diode into its own 470 µF capacitor (C3). A servo
current spike that sags +5V_SERVO for a few milliseconds cannot pull the logic rail down with it. The ESP32's LDO still
gets about 4.6 V. If brownouts appear anyway (`rst:0xf` / "Brownout detector" on the console), add a second MP1584 set to
5.0 V for +5V_LOGIC only (it costs about €1).

### Current budget — 12 V side (PSU 5 A, fuse 5 A)

| load | typical | peak / worst | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 37GB555 cocking, 5 m setting | — | **4.19 A** peak (3.66 A at 4 m) | calc/RESULTS.md §3 |
| 37GB555 start inrush / stall | — | **5.3 A** (12 V / 2.27 Ω) | calc/RESULTS.md §3 |
| MP1584 input (servos + logic) | ~0.3 A | ~1.4 A (buck at its 3 A limit) | 5 V × I / 0.88 / 12 V |
| buzzer + beacon | ~0.1-0.2 A | 0.2 A | assumed, check your parts |

- **Normal COCK at 5 m:** 4.19 A (motor) + ~0.5 A (escapement pre-staging during COCK) + 0.2 A (beacon) ≈ **4.9 A**.
  This is right at the PSU's 5 A rating, but it is a peak lasting tens of milliseconds within a 1.6 s cock. At 4 m it
  is about 4.4 A.
- **Motor stall:** 5.3 A + about 0.5 A ≈ **5.8 A (≈ 116 %)**. This lasts at most 200 ms grace + 150 ms window before the
  firmware stall detector stops the motor (`config.h` `kStallGraceMs`/`kStallWindowMs`). The GST60A series has overload
  protection with auto-recovering hiccup mode. Check the exact trip point on the datasheet; it was not verified here. If
  the PSU hiccups, everything resets and the firmware boots into COCKED_AT_BOOT/NOT_HOME, which is a safe state.
- **Fuse F1 5 A does not protect against a stall,** and it is not meant to. A 5 A blade fuse carries 5.8 A for seconds
  to minutes. F1 protects the wiring against a hard short (for example a pinched 12 V wire), in case the PSU's own
  protection fails. Stall protection is the firmware stall detector plus the timeouts (see firmware/README.md).
- **J1:** the common "DC-022B" panel jack is often rated only 3 A. Buy one explicitly rated **≥ 5 A**, or the jack becomes
  the hot spot.

### Current budget — 5 V side (MP1584 "3 A")

| load | typical | worst |
|---|---|---|
| MG996R, holding / idle | 10-200 mA | — |
| MG996R moving, light load (latch trip needs ≤ 0.21 N m vs ~1 N m stall) | 0.3-0.6 A | — |
| MG996R stalled | — | **~2.5 A each** (6 V figure; somewhat less at 5 V) |
| ESP32-C3 (through the SuperMini LDO) | ~0.1 A | 0.35 A WiFi TX peak |
| 2 x TCRT5000 module | ~50 mA | 70 mA |

The MP1584EN's "3 A" is a peak rating; for continuous load keep it under about 2.5 A (research/parts_notes.md §10).

- **One servo stalled:** 2.5 + 0.2 (other servo holding) + 0.45 (logic) ≈ **3.1 A**. This is at the limit. It can only
  happen through a mechanical jam or a servo pulse width calibrated against a hard stop. Calibrate `kLatch*Us`/`kEsc*Us`
  so neither servo sits stalled at an end position.
- **Both servos stalled at once:** about **5.5 A. This exceeds the MP1584.** The buck current-limits, +5V_SERVO
  collapses, and C3 holds the ESP32 for only about 1-2 ms, so the ESP32 browns out and resets. R2/R3 then switch the
  bridge off, and the firmware boots into a FAULT that does not move anything. The latch is self-locking (RESULTS §4),
  so a reset cannot fire the arm. **This is acceptable:** a double stall needs two simultaneous jams, the failure mode
  is safe, and the firmware sequence does not drive both servos under load at the same time. I checked
  `firmware/common/src/launcher_sm.cpp`:
  - The latch servo only changes position at ARMED→FIRE (line 391, TRIP) and back to HOLD (line 416). During COCK, FEED
    and ARMED it is held at HOLD.
  - The escapement moves in COCK (STAGE), FEED (RELEASE, then CLOSED at line 591) and is forced CLOSED in ARMED (line 380).
  - **Exceptions where both move:**
    1. At power-up both servos are commanded at once (`hal_esp32.cpp:88-91`).
    2. The escapement may still be closing when the latch trips (PINMAP.md finding 1).

    In both cases the loads are light, the realistic combined peak is about 1-1.5 A, and C2 (1000 µF low-ESR) absorbs
    the starting spikes.
- **If you see brownouts or the buck runs hot:** swap U1 for an XL4015 (5 A) module, or add the separate logic buck
  described above. Nothing else in the wiring needs to change.

## 2. Wire gauges (metric)

| run | current | wire |
|---|---|---|
| J1 → F1 → Q1 → TB1, TB1 → IBT-2 B+/B-, IBT-2 → motor | up to 5.3 A | **1.0 mm²** (≈ AWG18) silicone, red/black. A 1 m loop drops 0.17 V at 5 A |
| TB1 → MP1584 IN, TB1 → beacon/buzzer | ≤ 1.5 A | 0.5 mm² (≈ AWG20) |
| MP1584 OUT → +5V_SERVO distribution and its GND | ≤ 3 A | **0.75 mm²** (≈ AWG18-19) |
| servo leads | ≤ 2.5 A | stock servo cable (about 0.3 mm²). Keep it short; do not extend it with Dupont jumper wire |
| +5V_LOGIC → SuperMini 5V, TCRT VCC | ≤ 0.4 A | 0.25 mm² |
| all GPIO signals, switches, encoder, sensors | mA | 0.14-0.25 mm² (AWG26-24), twist each signal with its GND |
| Button: LiPo ↔ TP4056 ↔ SuperMini | ≤ 0.35 A peak | 0.25 mm² silicone |

## 3. Connectors

- **12 V power:** 5.08 mm screw terminals (TB1 and the IBT-2's own B+/B-/M+/M- screws). Use **ferrules** on stranded
  wire in every screw terminal. Do not use Dupont or JST anywhere on the 12 V / motor path.
- **Off-board sensors and switches:** **JST-XH 2.54 mm**. They are polarised and latching, so they will not shake loose
  when the arm fires. Use 2-pin for each limit switch, the test button and the safety-pin pole A; 3-pin for each
  TCRT5000 (VCC/GND/DO); 4-pin for the encoder (3V3/GND/A/B-unused).
- **Servos:** their stock 3-pin JR/Futaba plug onto a 3-pin male header on the perfboard. Mark the orientation: brown =
  GND, red = V+, orange = SIG. Fix it with a dab of hot glue or a printed clip.
- **SuperMini:** solder it to the perfboard on headers. Dupont jumpers are only acceptable for bench testing.
- **Safety-pin pole B** carries servo current (≤ 2.5 A). Use a microswitch rated ≥ 5 A (KW11 / SS-5 class) and 0.5 mm²
  wire.
- **Button:** solder everything; no connectors. The LiPo JST-PH plug may stay if the TP4056 board has a matching socket.

## 4. Wiring order (launcher)

1. **Bench-set the buck first.** Connect U1 MP1584 alone to the 12 V brick through F1 (clip leads are fine). Turn its
   trimmer until OUT reads **5.0 V ± 0.05 V** with no load. Then put a 10 Ω 5 W resistor across OUT, if you have one, and
   check it still reads ≥ 4.9 V. Unplug the brick.
2. Mount J1, F1 holder, Q1 (with R1 + ZD1 soldered directly at its legs) and TB1. Wire the input chain with 1.0 mm².
   Q1 orientation: **Drain to the fuse/jack side, Source to TB1**. The body diode must point from D to S so the circuit
   works before the gate turns on.
3. Wire TB1 → IBT-2 B+/B-. Solder C1 (1000 µF 25 V, observe polarity) and D1 SMBJ15A (cathode band to +12 V) **at the
   IBT-2 B+/B- terminals**.
4. Wire IBT-2 M+/M- → motor with 1.0 mm². Solder C4 100 nF ceramic **directly across the motor tabs**.
5. Wire TB1 → U1 IN+/IN-. Build the +5V_SERVO distribution: C2 1000 µF (polarity!), D3 1N5819 (band toward +5V_LOGIC),
   C3 470 µF. **Do not plug the SuperMini in yet.**
6. Wire the servo V+ lines: escapement servo straight from +5V_SERVO; latch servo through safety-pin pole B (NC).
7. Build the logic side on the perfboard:
   - R2/R3 10k pull-downs at RPWM/LPWM;
   - the IBT-2 VCC/R_EN/L_EN jumper to 3V3;
   - R4 10k pull-up and R5 100 Ω on GPIO2, Q2 AO3400, and the buzzer/beacon with D2 1N5819 (band to +12 V);
   - 100 nF caps on GPIO4/5/6/7;
   - R6/R7 1k + 10k pull-ups + BAT54 for the TCRT lines;
   - R8/R9 330 Ω in the servo signal lines.
8. Make up the JST-XH harnesses for the switches, safety pin pole A, test button, encoder (encoder VCC = **3V3**) and the
   TCRT modules (VCC = +5V_LOGIC).
9. Run the pre-power checklist (section 5). Only then fit the SuperMini.

## 5. Pre-power-on checklist (multimeter)

With **everything unplugged** (no brick, no USB):

- [ ] **No short between 12 V and GND:** resistance TB1 +12V ↔ GND is **> 1 kΩ**. It starts low and rises as C1 charges.
  A steady reading near 0 Ω means a short: find it.
- [ ] **No short between 5 V and GND:** +5V_SERVO ↔ GND > 1 kΩ (rises as C2 charges), and +5V_LOGIC ↔ GND likewise.
- [ ] **No short between 3V3 and GND** at the SuperMini socket.
- [ ] **Continuity (beep):**
  - J1 sleeve ↔ TB1 GND ↔ IBT-2 B- ↔ U1 IN- ↔ SuperMini GND ↔ every switch COM;
  - J1 tip ↔ F1 ↔ Q1 drain;
  - Q1 source ↔ TB1 +12V ↔ IBT-2 B+.
- [ ] **Polarity:** C1/C2/C3 stripe (–) to GND; D1 band to +12 V; D2 band to +12 V; D3 band toward the ESP32; BAT54
  cathode toward the TCRT module.
- [ ] **Q1 diode test:** red probe on D, black on S reads about 0.5 V (body diode). Reversed it reads OL.
- [ ] **Safety pin:**
  - pin inserted → GPIO7 pad ↔ GND reads 0 Ω, and +5V_SERVO ↔ latch-servo red wire reads open;
  - pin removed → the reverse.
- [ ] **Limit switches:** each GPIO pad ↔ GND reads 0 Ω only while its switch is actuated.

Then **power the 12 V brick with the SuperMini and both servos unplugged:**

- [ ] TB1 reads 11.8-12.3 V. With the brick plug reversed (if you have a reversible adapter), TB1 must read ~0 V.
- [ ] **+5V_SERVO reads 5.0 V ± 0.1 V BEFORE the ESP32 or any servo is connected.** Anything above 5.5 V will damage
  the SuperMini's LDO input path: re-trim U1.
- [ ] +5V_LOGIC reads about 4.6-4.8 V (5.0 V minus the D3 drop).
- [ ] The IBT-2 is cold and the motor does not move.

Then **unplug the brick, fit the SuperMini, flash it over USB with the brick still unplugged** (GPIO2's pull-up would
otherwise sound the beeper during flashing), and:

- [ ] Over USB only: run `status` on the console. Each switch toggles its input, the TCRT inputs change when a can is
  held in front of them, and `enc` counts when you turn the motor shaft by hand.
- [ ] Measure the TCRT DO line at the GPIO pad: HIGH ≤ 3.4 V, LOW ≤ 0.7 V.
- [ ] Unplug USB, plug the brick in, **safety pin inserted**. The beeper chirps briefly at boot, then goes quiet; the
  latch servo is unpowered (pin in).
- [ ] Remove the pin. The latch servo goes to HOLD. Plug in the servo horns only after you have checked the
  `kLatch*Us`/`kEsc*Us` endpoints with the horns off.
- [ ] First motor test: arm removed or springs unhooked. Watch for heat at Q1, J1, F1, IBT-2 and U1 after 10 cycles.

## 6. Button wiring order and checks

1. Desolder the SuperMini's red power LED.
2. Wire the LiPo to the TP4056 B+/B- (observe polarity: a reversed LiPo destroys the DW01A). Wire OUT+ → SuperMini 5V
   pin and OUT- → GND, both 0.25 mm².
3. Add:
   - the 1M/1M divider from OUT+ to GPIO1, with 100 nF at the midpoint;
   - the 1 MΩ pull-up GPIO3 → 3V3 and the microswitch GPIO3 → GND (optional 10 nF);
   - 1k → NPN base with 100k base → GND;
   - the coin motor between 3V3 and the collector, with a 1N5819 across it (band to 3V3);
   - 330 Ω → LED on GPIO5.
4. Checks:
   - before connecting the battery, OUT+ ↔ GND must read > 100 kΩ;
   - with the battery connected, GPIO1 reads VBAT/2 ± 2 %;
   - GPIO3 reads 3.3 V released and 0 V pressed.
5. **Flash with the LiPo unplugged.** Whether the SuperMini has a diode between USB VBUS and its 5V pin was not verified
   here. Without one, USB 5 V would be pushed into the LiPo through the TP4056 OUT pins.
6. Sleep current target: **≈ 50 µA** (calc/RESULTS.md §6). Measure it with the meter in series with B+. More than
   about 1 mA means the red LED is still on or something is not sleeping.
